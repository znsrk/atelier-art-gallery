02 / Atelier collection

Meet the perspectives.

Three fictional artists, each with a different way of looking. These personas help tell the story of our student collection.

01 / Earth & form

Mira Arden

Mira's imagined practice begins with the landscape: a hill, a warm afternoon, a simple horizon. Earthy colors and bold shapes turn familiar scenes into something quieter.

  • Palette: terracotta, olive, and cream
  • Focus: landscapes and geometric abstraction
See Quiet Sun ↗

02 / Light & space

Noah Vale

Noah's fictional collection explores the space between day and night. Cool blues, curved architecture, and open coastlines create a sense of distance and calm.

  • Palette: deep blue, sea green, and sand
  • Focus: abstract spaces and coastal landscapes
See Coastal Lines ↗

03 / Everyday poetry

Lena Sol

Lena's imagined studio is full of plants and ordinary objects. A branch, a vase, or a piece of fruit becomes a reason to slow down and notice the everyday.

  • Palette: soft peach, golden yellow, and leaf green
  • Focus: still life and botanical illustration
