A little about Atelier.

A student project with a simple idea: make art feel approachable, one page at a time.

The idea behind the gallery

Room for
a new perspective.

Atelier is an imaginary online gallery created for a web development midterm. We chose art because a gallery gives color, typography, and layout room to tell a story.

The collection uses original geometric illustrations. The artist profiles and exhibition themes are fictional, created to make the gallery feel connected and easy to explore.
